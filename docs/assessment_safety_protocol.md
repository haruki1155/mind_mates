# Assessment Safety-Screening Protocol (Disabled)

Status: **Not approved and not active.** The application must not present
suicide or self-harm screening questions until PACC and the research team give
written approval for this complete protocol. Placeholder contacts must never be
used in an active workflow.

Before activation, approval must cover:

- Exact, evidence-informed question wording and the population for which it is approved.
- A clear consent and privacy explanation shown before questions are presented.
- Immediate, reviewed response guidance for each supported risk level.
- Independently verified emergency, local crisis, and campus PACC contacts.
- Counselor notification targets, delivery time expectations, acknowledgment deadlines, and escalation ownership.
- After-hours handling, including who receives and acknowledges alerts when PACC is closed.
- Tamper-evident audit records for screening, notification, delivery, acknowledgment, and access; access must be role-limited and reviewed.
- Safe failure behavior when a notification service is unavailable. The user must receive verified direct-contact guidance, the failure must be audited, and the app must never imply that a counselor was notified.

Activation also requires privacy/security review, end-to-end failure testing,
staff training, documented operating hours, and a production readiness sign-off.
