Place licensed or royalty-free breathing audio assets here before release.
The feature works silently until the files are supplied.

Required assets:

- `ambient_calm_loop.mp3` — seamless, gentle background loop (2–5 minutes)
- `session_start.ogg` — short welcoming chime
- `inhale_cue.ogg` — subtle rising cue
- `exhale_cue.ogg` — subtle descending cue
- `hold_cue.ogg` — quiet hold cue
- `session_complete.ogg` — calm completion chime

Optional assets:

- `session_paused.ogg`
- `session_resumed.ogg`
- `save_success.ogg`
- `save_failed.ogg`

Avoid vocals, abrupt transients, and loop gaps. Keep cues below two seconds and
at a comfortable loudness relative to the background track.
